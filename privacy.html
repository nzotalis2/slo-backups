slo-backups: privacy policy

Effective 7 November 2026.

Who uses it

slo-backups is a personal tool used only by its owner, to back up the owner's own data to the owner's own Google Drive. No one else signs in to it.

What Google data it accesses

Only files it created itself in the owner's Google Drive, through Google's drive.file permission. It cannot read, list or change any other file. It does not access email, contacts, profile details or any other Google data.

How that data is used

It uploads backup files, checks that each upload is complete, and deletes its own copies once they are older than its retention period. It does nothing else with Google user data.

Sharing and storage

Nothing is shared with, sold to, or shown to anyone. No data is used for advertising or for training models. The sign-in token that lets it upload is stored only on the owner's server, readable only by the account that runs the backups.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's third-party connections page. Files it uploaded stay in the Drive until deleted there.

Contact

Questions: open an issue in this site's repository.
